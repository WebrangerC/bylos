The method I've chosen for managing MOCs is slightly complicated. I've envisioned two types of MOCs, "Activity" and "Topic".  Activity MOCs are productivity oriented and used for Projects and Spheres . Topic MOCs are knowledge oriented and related to Interests and Topics. 

I'm thinking that Interests are a part of PKM and not directly actionable.
